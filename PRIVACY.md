# Privacy policy: My Shortcut Keys for Gmail

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

My Shortcut Keys for Gmail does not collect, store, send or sell any personal data.

**What it does.** The extension runs only on `https://mail.google.com/`. It listens
for the keys you have set up and, when one is pressed, clicks the matching Gmail
button (for example Archive or Delete) or presses Gmail's own keyboard shortcut.
It does not read the content of your emails, contacts or any other Gmail data.

**What it stores.** Your shortcut settings (which key runs which action, and
whether shortcuts are on) are saved with the browser's extension storage
(`storage.sync`). If you are signed in to your browser with sync turned on, your
browser syncs these settings between your own devices. The developer never
receives them.

**Network.** The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no
analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.

**Permissions.**
- `storage`: saves your shortcut settings.
- Access to `mail.google.com`: runs the shortcuts inside Gmail.

**Contact.** Questions or problems: open an issue at
https://github.com/semicolonconsulting/mygmailshortcutkeys/issues
